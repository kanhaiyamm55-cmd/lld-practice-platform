# 5-Minute Demo

1. Open `demo.html`.
2. Click **View problem** on Parking Lot.
3. Click **Start practice**.
4. Add classes: `ParkingLot`, `ParkingSpot`, `Ticket`.
5. Give each class a responsibility.
6. Add interface `PricingStrategy` and purpose `Calculates parking fee`.
7. Add relationships: `ParkingLot -> Ticket`, `ParkingLot -> ParkingSpot`.
8. Explain why pricing is separated and mention one SOLID/trade-off point.
9. Submit and show the score breakdown.
10. Show **What went well** and **What to improve**.
11. Open **History** and click **Review**.
12. Click **Try again** to demonstrate the practice loop.
